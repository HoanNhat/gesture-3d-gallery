The Ghost of Svalbard

Arctic Apex Predator

Date Captured November 12, 2025
Field Location Svalbard Archipelago, Norway
Optics & Exposure Leica SL2 · Summicron 90mm f/2.0 · 1/1250s · ISO 100
Photographer / Curation Ethan Vale
Curatorial Field Note

A lone male polar bear traversing pack ice under the pale polar twilight. Observed for three days from drift ice at 79° North, moving in utter silence through sub-zero vapor with unmatched predatory grace.
